# Wrist Assured Support

Need help with Wrist Assured? Email unwarpedquads@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days.

## Common questions

**The app says it needs to download a speech model.**
On first use, tap **Download speech model** on the Home screen while on Wi-Fi. The model is several hundred MB. After that, transcription works offline.

**My entry says "Queued."**
iOS sometimes waits to process recordings made while the app is closed. Open the app and the entry will finish.

**Why don't I see AI summaries?**
Apple Intelligence features need an iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. On other iPhones, a built-in keyword engine still finds symptoms and habits.

**Weather is missing from an entry.**
Weather is added when your iPhone is online and Location access is on. Entries recorded offline get their weather when you're back online.

**How do I move my entries to a new iPhone?**
Before switching, open **Your data › Back up entries** and save the file to iCloud Drive or a computer. On the new iPhone, use **Restore from backup**. Restoring never deletes entries you already have.

**How do I delete my data?**
Delete single entries in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.
