# Wrist Assured Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 5, 2026

Wrist Assured ("the app") is made by Unwarped Quads LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your journal stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't collect it, see it, sell it or share it.

## 1. What we collect

**Nothing.** We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your recordings, transcripts, symptoms, health data, location or any other personal information.

## 2. What the app stores on your device

The app stores the following **only on your iPhone and Apple Watch**:

- Voice recordings you make in the app
- Transcripts, symptoms, moods, meals, habits, goals, tags, categories and notes made from those recordings
- Daily summaries, weekly reports and patterns
- Sleep, heart rate, heart rate variability and weather details added to each entry
- Your settings, including an optional PIN (stored only as a protected hash in your device's Keychain)

Speech recognition, Apple Intelligence analysis, summaries and pattern calculations all run on your device. Recordings move between your Apple Watch and iPhone directly through Apple's Watch Connectivity, not through our servers.

## 3. Apple Health

With your permission, the app **reads** sleep, heart rate variability, heart rate and resting heart rate from Apple Health to show context next to your entries and to find patterns. The app never writes to Apple Health. Health data stays on your device and is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never shared with anyone. You can change this permission at any time in **Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Wrist Assured**.

## 4. Location and weather

With your permission, the app uses your **approximate** location (rounded to about 1 km) to look up the weather for each entry through **Apple's WeatherKit** service. Only that rounded location and the time are sent to Apple. No recordings, transcripts or health data are sent. Apple handles this request under the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can turn off location access at any time in **Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Wrist Assured**. The app keeps working without weather.

## 5. Microphone

The app uses the microphone only while you are recording an entry. Recordings are saved on your device.

## 6. Speech model download

On first use, the app downloads a speech recognition model from **Hugging Face**, a third-party model host. This is a file download only. Like any web download, the host's servers see your device's IP address, but nothing about you or your journal is sent. After the download, transcription works offline.

## 7. Face ID

If you turn on Face ID lock, Face ID is handled entirely by iOS. The app never receives or stores your face data.

## 8. Exports and backups

Nothing leaves your device unless **you** choose to export it. When you export a PDF or text report, or a backup file, the app creates it on your iPhone and it goes only where you send it (for example Files, Mail, AirDrop or iCloud Drive). Once you share a file, the service you share it with handles it under its own privacy terms.

If you use iCloud Backup for your iPhone, Apple may include the app's data in your device backup under Apple's terms. We can't access those backups.

## 9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete a single entry in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side. You can withdraw any permission (Health, Location, Microphone) at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

## 10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## 11. Not medical advice

Wrist Assured is a personal wellness journal, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. Patterns show what was recorded together, not what causes it.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
